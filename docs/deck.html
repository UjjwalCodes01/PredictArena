02The productPrediction Leagues

A weekly league that scores
you like a forecaster.

Call Up or Down on BTC and ETH in fixed windows. Win to build a streak and climb the board. Two numbers do the real work.

Brier score

On a binary venue the price you pay is your claim: buying Up at 62c asserts Up is 62% likely. Brier is the mean squared error between what you claimed and what happened.

Edge

Your win rate minus the average price the market charged you. Back Up at 60c, win 70% of the time, and your edge is +10.0 — you are finding sides the market underprices.

Brier alone rewards buying heavy favourites, which is confidence, not skill. Edge is the honest one, because luck averages to zero over enough calls. Both appear after five settled calls — below that they are noise, and the board shows a dash rather than a number nobody should trust.

04Event ContractsPrediction Leagues

How we use DreamDEX
Event Contracts.

Fourteen SDK calls behind one module. packages/dex is the only seam to the venue — the UI and the indexer never reach the network directly.

Discover listBinaryVenueIds · listBinaryAssets · listLiveBinaryMarkets · getMarketOnchain Venues and contract addresses are resolved at runtime, never hard-coded — ids move. Tradability is a chain read, so it is a fact rather than an indexer guess.
Price getBinaryOrderBook · getBinaryBookParams Every price is quantized to the venue's own tick and lot grids. All amounts are bigint end to end — a lint rule fails CI if a float appears near money.
Place buildPlaceOrder · placeOrder · createTrader · faucet The browser signs; we hold no key. IOC by default, so a thin book cancels instead of failing hard — liquidity gaps at window rolls are measured reality here.
Settle getOutcomeBalance · getClaimable · redeem Winnings are claimed, not received, so the UI says so. Status is an enum — PENDING·WON·LOST·VOID·FAILED — because a void is an outcome, not an error.

Two we got wrong first
Approvals must cover quantity, not escrow — a binary contract settles at up to 1.0 collateral. And ImmediateOrCancelNoFill arrives as a bare selector, not a message. Both are now pinned by tests, and both are written up for the DreamDEX team.

05The AI playerPrediction Leagues

An AI that plays —
and can lose in public.

Every AI in this space advises you, and an assistant's track record is whatever its author claims. Ours holds its own wallet, places real orders through the same code path as the button you press, and is ranked on the same board by the same Brier and edge.

It passes, mostly

It trades only when its estimate beats the book by a margin that widens when its own confidence is low. A forecaster that bets every window is a coin flip with a rationale attached.

It shows its working

Every estimate is recorded before the window closes, with the prices the market was showing at that moment, and never revised.

It cannot flatter itself

Whether it won is read from the chain by the same function that scores every human. No branch anywhere could report otherwise.

Runs on Google Cloud Vertex AI or the Claude API. Short-horizon crypto direction is close to a coin flip and the model is told so — the claim is not that it wins. The claim is that it is measured, publicly, by the same yardstick as everyone else.
